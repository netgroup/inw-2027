Hotel Carlo Magno

INW 2027 will take place at the Carlo Magno Hotel SPA Resort, Via Cima Tosa 26, Campo Carlo Magno, Madonna di Campiglio.

The hotel is fully booked. The room allocation reserved for workshop participants is no longer available.

How to get there

By car

From Milan, take the A4 motorway to Brescia Est, then follow signs for Lago d’Idro, Tione di Trento and Madonna di Campiglio. From Verona, take the A22 motorway towards Trento and continue via Sarche and Tione di Trento.

By train and bus

The nearest main railway station is Trento. From the nearby bus station, Trentino Trasporti services connect Trento, Tione, Madonna di Campiglio and Campo Carlo Magno. Check the winter timetable before travelling.
