Call for contributions

Submission deadline: 20 November 2026

Open submission form

The 23rd Italian Networking Workshop welcomes recent results, work in progress and new research ideas on all aspects of communication networks and related systems.

Contributions may be presented as a research talk, with a 15-minute oral presentation, or as a poster during the dedicated poster session.

Contributions are not included in published proceedings and are not copyrighted by the workshop. Authors may therefore submit the work to other appropriate venues.

Topics

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Beyond 5G and 6G networks
  • Wireless and mobile networks
  • mm-Wave communications
  • Non-terrestrial networks
  • Network architectures and protocols
  • Routing and channel access
  • Information-centric networking
  • Optical networks
  • Data-center and cloud networking
  • Software-defined networks
  • Edge computing
  • Internet of Things and sensor networks
  • Vehicular networks
  • Network measurement and analysis
  • Traffic engineering and control
  • Multimedia and video streaming
  • Network security and privacy
  • Energy-efficient networking
  • Semantic and effective communications
  • Performance evaluation
