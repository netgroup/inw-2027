About INW

A meeting place for the Italian networking research community.

The Italian Networking Workshop brings together researchers, PhD students and professionals to discuss ongoing work, exchange ideas and foster new collaborations in an informal setting.

Organized by GTTI, with local organization by the University of Rome Tor Vergata.
