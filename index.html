23rd Italian
Networking Workshop

19–21 January 2027
Madonna di Campiglio

Research, discussion and new collaborations.

The main purposes of the Italian Networking Workshop are:

  • to present recent results and receive immediate feedback from the community;
  • to offer PhD students and young researchers an opportunity to meet colleagues and discuss scientific topics;
  • to foster collaborations and the development of new project proposals;
  • to do all this in an informal and comfortable setting.

Contributions are not published or copyrighted by the workshop and may be submitted to appropriate international venues.

INW 2027 is organized by GTTI, with local organization by the University of Rome Tor Vergata.
