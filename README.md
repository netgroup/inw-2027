# INW 2027

The 23rd Italian Networking Workshop will take place in Madonna di Campiglio on 19–21 January 2027.
INW brings together the Italian networking research community in an informal and collaborative setting.
The workshop welcomes recent results, work in progress and new research ideas.
The event is organized by GTTI, with local organization by the University of Rome Tor Vergata.
Website: [inw2027.netgroup.uniroma2.it](https://inw2027.netgroup.uniroma2.it/)
